Candidate Criteria

Eligibility Criteria

Freshers and final year students are eligible to apply.

Eligible If:

Academic Score

Minimum 50% in Graduation (10+2+3 pattern compulsory).
No minimum percentage criteria for 10th and 12th!
Final year graduation students awaiting results are also eligible.

Age Limit

Between 21 to 30 years of age at application date.

Experience

Work experience is NOT required. Fresh graduates welcome.

Not Eligible If:

Relative Policy

Immediate relative currently employed at Axis Bank.

Age Exceeded

Age is below 21 years or above 30 years.

Disciplinary / Criminal Action

Convicted under criminal statutes or with pending legal actions.
