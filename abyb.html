Candidate Criteria

Clear & Inclusive Eligibility Criteria

Fresh graduates are encouraged to apply! No prior work experience needed.

Eligible If You Meet:

Academic Qualification

Minimum 50% in Graduation (10+2+3 pattern compulsory).
No minimum percentage criteria for 10th and 12th!
Final year graduation students awaiting results are also eligible.

Age Limit

Must be between 21 to 30 years of age at the time of application.

Work Experience

Work experience is NOT an eligibility criterion! Freshers are welcome. Candidates with prior work experience are also eligible.

Not Eligible If:

Conflict of Interest

Immediate relative currently working at Axis Bank (in accordance with Axis Bank relative hiring policy).

Age Out of Range

Age is below 21 years or above 30 years at application date.

Disciplinary Action

Subjected to disciplinary proceedings or termination for misconduct by previous employers.

Criminal / Legal Proceedings

Convicted under criminal statutes or with pending legal actions.

Original graduation marksheets and degree certificate must be submitted at the time of campus joining.
