Candidate Qualification

Are You Eligible to Apply?

Axis Bank follows stringent, merit-based eligibility criteria for Commercial Banking Group roles.

Eligible If You Meet:

Age Bracket

Must be between 22 to 28 years at the time of batch admission.

Academic Minimums

Graduation or Post-Graduation from any stream with minimum 50% marks. 10th, 12th, and Degree must be completed in regular mode with at least 50% in each.

Work Experience
  • Graduates: Minimum 1 to 2 years of work experience in sales, service, or customer relationship domains.
  • Post Graduates (MBA / M.Com etc.): Minimum 6 months of corporate experience.
  • Relieving letter from employer is required prior to joining campus tenure in Pune.
Communication Skills

Strong verbal & written English proficiency, executive presence, and aptitude for commercial client handling.

Not Eligible If:

Relative Hiring Policy

You have a direct relative currently employed at Axis Bank. If two relatives apply simultaneously, the candidate with the lower score must withdraw.

Disciplinary Proceedings

You have ever been subjected to disciplinary proceedings or termination for misconduct by any previous employer.

Legal or Criminal History

You have been prosecuted, convicted under any criminal law, or have legal action pending in a consumer forum or court of law.

Incomplete Documentation

Unable to provide verified PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, 10th, 12th, and Degree original marksheets and valid ID proofs.

Please ensure your profile complies with the above policies before submitting your application fee.

Graduates (22–28 years old) with at least 1 to 2 years of work experience, or Postgraduates with a minimum of 6 months of work experience in sales or service domains. Applicants should have a minimum of 50% marks in 10th, 12th, and Degree, good English communication skills, and an aptitude for corporate and commercial client engagement.

The selection process consists of: (1) Online Registration with ₹299 fee, (2) An 80-minute online proctored assessment covering Cognitive, Written English, and SpeechX verbal competencies, (3) Virtual panel interview with Adda247 Skills, and (4) Final interview with Axis Bank senior panel. Selected candidates receive an official Provisional Offer Letter from Axis Bank.

The 12-month program is structured as follows:
• Months 1 & 2: Online instructor-led virtual campus training from your home.
• Months 3 & 4: Residential in-campus training at our premier training campus in Pune (boarding, lodging & training facilities provided).
• Months 5 & 6: Live branch internship at Axis Bank branches.
• Months 7 to 12: On-the-Job Training (OJT) as Deputy Manager at assigned Axis Bank branches.

Upon successful completion of all academic and practical modules, you will be awarded a recognized Post Graduate Diploma in Commercial Banking from a partner university, alongside your permanent placement confirmation as Deputy Manager at Axis Bank.

You will receive your Provisional Offer Letter directly from Axis Bank after clearing the interview stages, before you pay any program admission fees. Upon successful completion of the training, you will be permanently confirmed on the rolls of Axis Bank.

The total program fee is ₹2,25,000 + 18% GST (Total: ₹2,65,500). This covers comprehensive online coursework, 2 months residential campus training in Pune (including accommodation, meals, course kits, and mentor sessions), university examination & diploma certification fees, and dedicated placement onboarding.

You only pay the nominal ₹299 registration fee at the time of application. The main program fee is payable strictly after you receive your Provisional Offer Letter from Axis Bank. It can be paid in milestones: ₹25,000 + GST on accepting the offer letter, ₹1,00,000 + GST before batch start, and ₹1,00,000 + GST before the campus tenure. Alternatively, you can finance the entire remaining ₹2,00,000 + GST through an Axis Bank education loan.

Axis Bank provides an education loan for ₹2,00,000 + GST at an interest rate of 13% p.a. You only need to pay the initial ₹25,000 + GST enrollment fee yourself. The loan requires no collateral, and repayment EMIs start 6 months AFTER you start earning your salary as an Axis Bank employee. The EMI is automatically deducted from your monthly salary.

From Month 5 onward (branch internship & OJT), you will receive regular monthly salary payouts. You will earn approximately ₹3,50,000 over the course of the 12-month program.

The starting gross CTC ranges from ₹6.3 to ₹6.8 LPA (depending on whether you are posted in a Metro or Non-Metro location).
